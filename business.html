Business & Building

I do not present myself as a founder before I have built the company. I do, however, take seriously the direction I am heading.

An emerging chapter

I intend to build companies. The exact vehicle may evolve, but I am interested in ventures at the intersection of useful technology, health, information, and real-world problems.

How I think about building

My background has trained me to move between evidence, communication, technology and execution. I want to carry that multidisciplinary approach into entrepreneurship.

The principle

Aut viam inveniam aut faciam. I shall either find a way or make one.

Business belongs on this site as a statement of trajectory: ambitious, explicit, and honest about what is still being built.
