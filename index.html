01 / About

I have never been interested in fitting neatly into one box. I am interested in becoming, building, and solving difficult problems.

I am a Nigerian-trained medical doctor whose work has expanded across clinical medicine, research, artificial intelligence, scientific communication, digital publishing and search strategy.

The disciplines look different, but the instinct behind them is the same: understand the problem deeply, communicate clearly, and find a way forward. Business is the next chapter I intend to build. Not a claim about what already exists, but a direction I am deliberately growing toward.

03 / Research

Questions worth pursuing.

My research experience spans clinical and public-health questions, with a growing interest in how data and computational methods can strengthen health research.

2023

COVID-19 Vaccine Uptake Among Doctors

Cross-sectional undergraduate research assessing vaccine uptake and barriers among doctors at the University of Nigeria Teaching Hospital, analysed in IBM SPSS.

2021

CKD Progression & APOL1 Nephropathy

Supported a prospective cohort study through biological-sample processing, dietary-recall interviews, participant follow-up and protocol implementation.

2019–21

Smartphone Use Among University Students

Contributed to a cross-sectional study of 408 students with the Medical Research and Humanitarian Society, UNTH, and helped run SPSS training for 700+ members.
